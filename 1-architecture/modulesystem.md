# modulesystem

ModuleSystem is Nintendo's game engine for its modern mainline games, including OOT (Owl), TotK, Mario Wonder, and Splatoon 3. (inferred)

The games are known to share it because their filesystems follow the same layout. Among a great many more folders, each has `Pack/` (containing `Actor/`), `System/Resource/` (home of the [[restbl]]), and many many more. (confirmed)

The engine is far too large for one page, so its layers get their own pages under `1-architecture`, and this one only says what ModuleSystem is.
